Maeumlog Privacy Policy

Effective: September 29, 2026

US residents: see also our Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy.
This policy is available in English and Korean. If you live outside Korea, the English version controls. If you live in Korea, the Korean version controls.

Mauve Labs ("we," "us") explains in this policy how Maeumlog, our mood journal app (the "Service"), handles your personal information.

The short version

  • If you don't sign in, or you've never had Premium, your entries, notes, and photos stay on your device (and in your device's own backups, if you've turned them on). We never receive them.
  • With Premium, only entries dated within your Premium period are backed up to our servers so you can sync them across devices.
  • We don't sell your data, show ads, or use your entries for analytics, profiling, or AI training. We don't access your entries except when you ask us to (for example, for support) or the law requires it.

2. How long we keep data, and how we delete it

  • Account deletion: when you delete your account, you're signed out right away, and the name, email, and profile photo shown in the app are removed immediately. If you sign back in with the same account within 30 days, the deletion is canceled. After 30 days, we permanently delete your account information (email, name, sign-in connection), the entries, photos, and profile photo stored on our servers, your settings, and your consent records. You can ask us to delete everything immediately instead of waiting 30 days.
  • When Premium ends: if a subscription expires, is refunded, or otherwise ends, we keep your server entries and photos for 1 year from the end date and then permanently delete them. If you start Premium again within that year, they won't be deleted. This doesn't apply to lifetime access. It only affects our server copy, not the data on your device.
  • Records we're required to keep: the following are disconnected from your account, stored separately from other personal information, and deleted when the period ends: contract and cancellation records (5 years), payment and delivery records (5 years), and consumer complaint and dispute records (3 years), as required by Korea's Act on the Consumer Protection in Electronic Commerce.
  • How we delete: electronic files are deleted so they can't be recovered. Server copies are deleted by an automated process that runs daily. Deleted data may remain in our hosting provider's backup and log systems for a short period (up to 30 days) until it's overwritten. Data on your device is deleted by your phone's operating system when you delete the app.

3. Sharing with third parties

We don't sell or share your personal information with third parties, except with your consent, or where the law requires it or law enforcement requests it through legally required procedures. When we verify a purchase, we look up the transaction with Apple or Google; that's a check of the purchase you made in their store. If you ask Apple for a refund of an App Store purchase and Apple asks us for usage information, we'll share how much the purchase was used only if you've agreed to it.

Other parties data flows to or from: when you sign in, the provider you choose (Google, Naver, Kakao, or Apple) handles the sign-in and gives us your account ID, email, and name. Purchases are processed by Apple or Google. Their own privacy policies apply to what they handle.

Nevada residents: we don't sell personal information. You can send a sale opt-out request to mauvelabs@mauvelabsinc.com.

5. Children

You must be 14 or older to create an account (Terms of Service, Section 4). Maeumlog isn't directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. If we learn that we've collected personal information from a child under 14, we'll delete it promptly. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we'll delete it. For users who aren't signed in, we process only the crash report, abuse prevention, and server log data described in Section 1.

6. Sensitive information and automated decisions

  • Your mood entries and notes can reveal information about your mental health. We treat them as sensitive: we process them only to store and sync them at your request, we don't analyze them, and we protect them as described in Section 8. If you prefer, you can keep entries only on your device by not signing in or not using Premium.
  • We don't use pseudonymized data, and we don't make decisions that significantly affect you using fully automated systems.

7. Cookies and tracking

The app doesn't use web cookies. Because we don't track you across third-party apps or websites, we don't change how we handle your data in response to browser "Do Not Track" or Global Privacy Control signals. Crash reports can be turned off in Settings (Section 1).

8. How we protect your data

  • Administrative: we limit who can access personal information and follow internal procedures.
  • Technical: encryption in transit (TLS); per-account access control (row-level security) so other users can't access your data; admin keys stored separately from the app; encrypted storage of sign-in data on your device; removal of personal information from crash reports; least-privilege access to server functions.
  • Physical: protected under our provider's (Supabase) data center security.

9. Your rights

  • You can ask to access, correct, or delete your personal information, to stop processing, or to withdraw consent at any time.
  • In the app: view your info on the Profile screen (access), change your profile photo (correction), Profile > Settings > Delete account (deletion), turn off Settings > Send crash reports (restrict processing), and sign out (stop syncing).
  • For anything else, email us (Section 10). If you can't use the app, use our account deletion page. We aim to act on your request and let you know the result within 10 days, and in any case within the time required by law.
  • You can also exercise your rights through a legal representative or an authorized agent, who will need to provide a signed authorization.
  • We can't delete information we're legally required to keep (Section 2). If we refuse a request, we'll tell you why and how to appeal.
  • We don't discriminate against you for exercising your privacy rights.
