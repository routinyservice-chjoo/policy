DailyCumulus: Ad-Free Mood Diary

DailyCumulus (formerly Maeumlog) is a mood journal with no ads. Once a day, tap Mongle, pick how you feel, and you're done. Add feelings, activities, a note, or a photo only when you want to.

No ads. Ever.

No banners, no pop-ups, no "watch this to unlock." Nothing gets between you and what you're writing.

Your entries stay private

  • On the free plan, your entries never leave your phone
  • With Premium, entries are encrypted in transit and stored on servers in Seoul, South Korea, so you can sync them across devices
  • We don't sell your data, show ads, or use your entries for analytics or AI training
  • Delete your account right in the app, anytime

Get the app

DailyCumulus is available for iPhone on the App Store and for Android on Google Play.
