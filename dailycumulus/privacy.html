Mauve Labs ("we," "us") explains in this policy how DailyCumulus, our mood journal app (the "Service"), handles your personal information.

The short version

  • If you don't sign in, or you've never had Premium, your entries, notes, and photos stay on your device (and in your device's own backups, if you've turned them on). We never receive them.
  • With Premium, only entries dated within your Premium period are backed up to our servers so you can sync them across devices. While you have Premium, your app settings and the feelings and activities you create (names and icons) also sync, including ones you made before Premium.
  • On Android, if you installed the app by tapping our ad on Google, we ask once whether we can let Google know that you opened the app, so we can see whether our ad worked. Nothing is sent unless you agree. Nothing is sent on iPhone.
  • We don't sell your data, show ads, or use your entries for analytics, profiling, or AI training. We don't access your entries except when you ask us to (for example, for support) or the law requires it.

  • We don't analyze your entries or use them for advertising, profiling, or AI training, and we don't access them except when you ask us to or the law requires it.
  • Before a crash report is sent, we strip out entry content, notes, photos, email, name, and IP address. You can turn crash reports off anytime in Settings > Send crash reports. Turning them off doesn't limit anything in the app.
  • Usage stats never include your mood, feelings, activities, notes, photos, email, name, or account ID, and they aren't linked to your account. We don't use an outside analytics service — they're stored only on our own server (Supabase, Section 4). Google ad measurement (below) is separate from usage stats. If the App Store or Google Play tells us you're under 18, we don't collect them at all. On iPhone, we collect them only if you turn on Settings > Share anonymous usage stats. On Android, you can turn them off there anytime. Leaving them off doesn't limit anything in the app.
  • Handled only on your device (never sent to us): (1) Mongle Insights, stats, and your yearly report are calculated on your device. (2) To show your widget, today's mood and your streak are saved in storage shared by the app and its widgets on your device (an App Group on iPhone). If app lock is on, your mood isn't saved there and doesn't appear on the widget. We never receive what your widget shows — anonymous usage stats only count how many widgets you have, by size (above). (3) Share cards, yearly report images, and PDFs are created on your device and never include your notes or photos. They go only to the app you choose, and that app's policies apply from there. (4) A backup of your entries is created on your device as one zip file with your dates, moods, feelings, activities, notes, and the photos on this device. It goes only where you choose to send or save it, such as the Files app, email, or a cloud drive. That app's or service's policies apply from there, and we never receive the file. When you restore from a backup file (including a CSV backup made with an older version of the app), the file you pick is read only on your device, and restored entries are treated like any other entry (entries dated within your Premium period are synced). (5) Smart reminder times are worked out on your device from when you've recently logged.
  • We don't collect advertising IDs (IDFA or the Android advertising ID). We also don't track you across other companies' apps or websites, with one exception: on Android, if you installed the app from our Google ad and you agree, we let Google know once that you opened the app so we can measure that ad (see "Google ad measurement" below).
  • Google ad measurement (Android only): if you installed the app on Android by tapping a DailyCumulus ad on Google, the app asks you once, when you first open it (or the next time you open it, if you were offline), whether it's OK to let Google know you opened the app. Nothing is sent unless you agree, and the app works the same either way. If you agree, the app sends the items listed in the table above to Google (Google Ads) one time, through our server. We don't collect or send your advertising ID (that field is sent as all zeros), and we don't send your entries, notes, photos, account information, or email. Google requires your device's IP address, so our server passes it along. Our server doesn't store the ad click ID or your IP address. To block duplicate or abusive requests, it keeps only a one-way hash of your IP address and a one-way hash of a random install number created just for this purpose, in a request counter that's deleted within about a day. That install number is separate from the anonymous usage-stats ID and isn't sent to Google. To say no, tap "No thanks" on that screen, and nothing is sent. Because the data is sent only once, we can't take back what Google has already received. You can contact us with questions (Section 10), and you can manage how Google uses your data for ads with Google's tools, such as My Ad Center (myadcenter.google.com). Nothing is sent on iPhone.

If you installed the app after seeing an ad, Apple lets that ad network know about the install without identifying you. We don't receive or send any identifier for you in this process.
  • Legal bases (Korean Personal Information Protection Act): performing our contract with you (account, sync, purchases, consent records), complying with legal obligations (purchase records), our legitimate interests in keeping the Service secure, working, and compliant with law (crash reports, anonymous usage stats on Android, abuse prevention, server logs, the parental consent check), and your consent (anonymous usage stats on iPhone, and Google ad measurement on Android).

2. How long we keep data, and how we delete it

  • Account deletion: when you delete your account, you're signed out right away, and the name, email, and profile photo shown in the app are removed immediately. If you sign back in with the same account within 30 days, the deletion is canceled. After 30 days, we permanently delete your account information (email, name, sign-in connection), the entries, photos, and profile photo stored on our servers, the feelings and activities you created, your settings, your consent records, and other data linked to your account (purchase records are handled as described below). You can ask us to delete everything immediately instead of waiting 30 days.
  • When Premium ends: if a subscription expires, is refunded, or otherwise ends, we keep your server entries and photos (including your profile photo) for 1 year from the end date and then permanently delete them. If you start Premium again within that year, they won't be deleted. This doesn't apply to lifetime access. It only affects our server copy, not the data on your device. The feelings and activities you created and your app settings aren't part of this deletion; they're deleted with your account information 30 days after you delete your account.
  • Records we're required to keep: as required by Korea's Act on the Consumer Protection in Electronic Commerce, we keep contract and cancellation records and payment and delivery records for 5 years from the purchase date, and consumer complaint and dispute records for 3 years, and then delete them. When your account is permanently deleted, your purchase records stay where they are, with only the link to your account removed. They're linked to an account again only if you sign up again with the same store account (Apple ID or Google account) and restore your purchases.
  • How we delete: electronic files are deleted so they can't be recovered. Server copies are deleted by an automated process that runs daily. Deleted data may remain in our hosting provider's backup and log systems for a short period (up to 30 days) until it's overwritten. Data on your device is deleted by your phone's operating system when you delete the app.

If you ask Apple for a refund of an App Store purchase and Apple asks us for usage information, we'll share how much the purchase was used only if you've agreed to it.

5. Children

You must be 14 or older to create an account (Terms of Service, Section 4). DailyCumulus isn't directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. If we learn that we've collected personal information from a child under 14, we'll delete it promptly. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we'll delete it. For users who aren't signed in, we process only the crash report, anonymous usage stats (under the conditions in Section 1), Google ad measurement (only if you agree, Section 1), abuse prevention, and server log data described in Section 1.

6. Sensitive information and automated decisions

  • Your mood entries and notes can reveal information about your mental health. We treat them as sensitive: we process them only to store and sync them at your request, we don't analyze them, and we protect them as described in Section 8. If you prefer, you can keep entries only on your device by not signing in or not using Premium.
  • We don't use pseudonymized data, and we don't make decisions that significantly affect you using fully automated systems.

7. Cookies and tracking

The app doesn't use web cookies. Because we don't track you across third-party apps or websites, we don't change how we handle your data in response to browser "Do Not Track" or Global Privacy Control signals. Crash reports can be turned off in Settings. Anonymous usage stats are sent on iPhone only if you turn them on, and can be turned off on Android (Section 1). Google ad measurement on Android is sent only if you agree (Section 1).

8. How we protect your data

  • Administrative: we limit who can access personal information and follow internal procedures.
  • Technical: encryption in transit (TLS); per-account access control (row-level security) so other users can't access your data; admin keys stored separately from the app; encrypted storage of sign-in data on your device; removal of personal information from crash reports; least-privilege access to server functions.
  • Physical: protected under our provider's (Supabase) data center security.

9. Your rights

  • You can ask to access, correct, or delete your personal information, to stop processing, or to withdraw consent at any time.
  • In the app: view your info on the Profile screen (access), change your profile photo (correction), Profile > Settings > Delete account (deletion), turn off Settings > Send crash reports (restrict processing), and sign out (stop syncing).
  • For anything else, email us (Section 10). If you can't use the app, use our account deletion page. We aim to act on your request and let you know the result within 10 days, and in any case within the time required by law.
  • You can also exercise your rights through a legal representative or an authorized agent, who will need to provide a signed authorization.
  • We can't delete information we're legally required to keep (Section 2). If we refuse a request, we'll tell you why and how to appeal.
  • We don't discriminate against you for exercising your privacy rights.
